Lagrange Gate

Pilot a Space Telescope · Support

Lagrange Gate is a game for iPad in which you keep a space telescope balanced at L2, the gravitational saddle 1.5 million kilometers beyond Earth.

Getting started

Choose Tutorial on the title screen. Flight School teaches every control in about five minutes, and the station can't be lost while you learn. Most lessons have Show me, which flies the maneuver for you first.

Common questions

I can't hear anything. Check that Silent Mode is off: swipe down from the top-right corner of the screen and make sure the bell is not switched on. Then check Controls & sound on the title screen, where sound can be switched on and each volume set.

How do I stop spinning? Tap the slew button that pulses to stop the spin, then turn back until the sunshield faces the Sun. (On Cadet nothing spins the station.)

Slew stopped working. The reaction wheels are full. Hold DESAT for about a second to empty them. It costs some propellant.

Where are my records kept? Only on your iPad. Deleting the game deletes them. Reset records, under Controls & sound on the title screen, erases them on purpose.

Where is the science lesson? The Science lesson tab on the title screen.
