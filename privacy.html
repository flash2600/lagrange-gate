Lagrange Gate

Privacy policy · Effective October 18, 2026

Lagrange Gate does not collect any information about you, and shares nothing with anyone.

What the game does not do

It has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It includes no third-party software that gathers data.

What stays on your device

Your best scores, completed missions and settings (such as sound volume) are saved on your device so the game can show them to you. They never leave it. To delete them, use Reset records under Controls & sound, or delete the game. Because nothing is sent to us, there is nothing for us to keep or to delete.

This page

The game's Privacy policy button opens this page in your web browser. The page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard records of visits to it under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Children

Lagrange Gate is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

Future versions may have modified privacy policies which will be updated on this page.
