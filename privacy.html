Lagrange Gate

Privacy policy · Effective October 18, 2026

Lagrange Gate does not collect, store or share any information about you.

What the game does not do

It has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It includes no third-party software that gathers data.

What stays on your device

Your best scores, completed missions and settings (such as sound volume) are saved on your device so the game can show them to you. They never leave it. Deleting the game deletes them.

Children

Because the game collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from children.

Changes

Future versions may have modified privacy policies which will be updated on this page.
